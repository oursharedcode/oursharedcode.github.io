How did the developer pass the exam?

I built a set of free English-preparation books after going through the exam myself. They're free to download, with no sign-up and no account, at freeieltsbooks.net.

This page is mine personally — it isn't part of that site, and it isn't affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to IELTS, the British Council, IDP, or Cambridge Assessment English.

If one of the books helped you and you'd like to support the work, you can leave a voluntary tip here. It only ever goes toward hosting and domain costs — nothing is sold, and it isn't required to use any of the books.
