Privacy Policy

For www.oursharedcode.com. Last updated 19 August 2026.

The short version: this site has no accounts, no sign-ups, and no database. Nothing you type into the tools here is sent to us or stored on a server. The one thing that does involve third parties is advertising, and this page explains exactly what that means and how to switch it off.

Who runs this site

OurSharedCode is an independent personal project. There is no company behind it. You can reach the operator at plusminusproject@gmail.com.

What we collect directly

Nothing. There is no registration, no login, no contact form, no newsletter, and no analytics on this site. We do not ask for your name, your email address, or any other personal detail, and we have no database in which to keep one.

Hosting

The site is served as static files by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives the technical information your browser sends in order to deliver a page — including your IP address, the page requested, and your browser type — and may keep it in server logs for security and operational purposes. That processing is GitHub's, not ours, and we have no access to those logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Prompt Engineering Studio

The tool at /prompt-engineering-studio/ runs entirely inside your browser. The prompts you write, the custom blocks you create, and your saved library are held in your browser's localStorage on your own device, and files you save go to a location you choose on your own disk. None of it is transmitted to us or to anyone else — there is no server component to transmit it to. Clearing your browser storage erases it, and we cannot recover it, because we never had a copy.

Advertising cookies

Some pages on this site show ads served by Google AdSense. Pages that do not contain ad code — including /free-english-books/ — do not run any advertising script at all.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices or youronlinechoices.com.

Opting out does not remove ads; it makes them non-personalised. You can also block cookies in your browser settings, which this site will tolerate without breaking.

Visitor map

The studio page may display a small world map showing roughly where recent visitors came from, provided by a third-party widget. It derives an approximate location from the IP address of the request. It does not identify you, and we see only aggregate dots on a map — never a list of individuals.

Donation links

The page at /free-english-books/ links to Ko-fi and Buy Me a Coffee. Those links open on their sites, not ours. Any payment happens entirely on their platform under their own privacy policy and their own terms; we never see your card details, and we receive no personal information about you from them. Tipping is voluntary and nothing on this domain is sold.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct, or delete on request — a request we would be unable to fulfil for the simple reason that we have nothing on file. Rights you have under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, or the CCPA regarding data held by Google or by GitHub should be directed to those companies, whose policies are linked above.

Children

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will appear at this address with a new date at the top. Material changes — for instance, introducing analytics — will be described here rather than made quietly.
