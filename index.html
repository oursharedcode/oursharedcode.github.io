Why it works this way

Every one of these started as something I needed and could not find for free, or could not find without handing over an email address first. Giving them away costs me hosting and a domain, which is a small price for work that was going to exist anyway.

None of it needs to know who you are, so none of it asks. The books download without an account. The studio keeps your work in your own browser and sends it nowhere. If something here saves you an afternoon, that is the whole of what it was for.
